sentence_accuracy_tol2

sentence_accuracy_tol2 = (1/N) Σ 1[D(norm(referenceᵢ), norm(predictionᵢ)) ≤ 2]

允许每句最多两个字符编辑错误的句级准确率，越高越好。

CER

CER = Σ D(norm(referenceᵢ), norm(predictionᵢ)) / Σ max(1, |norm(referenceᵢ)|)

CER是字符错误率，不是词错误率，越低越好。

Token loss

L = -(1/T) Σ log p(yₜ | y<ₜ, audio)
